Explanation:
The results are inconsistent because of a TOC-TOU (Time-of-Check to Time-of-Use)
vulnerability. Multiple bot processes read the inventory value at nearly the same time
before any of them writes back the updated value. This means several processes see the
same inventory count, all approve their purchases simultaneously, and all subtract from
the same original value — causing the inventory to go negative or leave unexpected
remainders. The final result depends entirely on how the OS scheduler interleaves the
processes, which is why each run produces a different value.
